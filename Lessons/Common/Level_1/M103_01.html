EO M103.01 - Identify the Responsibilities of a Follower in a Team

Overview

This lesson helps cadets recognize their responsibilities as followers in a team. They will learn a total of nine responsibilities of being a follower.

Teaching Points

TP1 Explain the following four responsibilities of a follower, to include:
a. respect the leader and other team members;
b. cooperate with others;
c. admit mistakes and learn from experience; and
d. accept constructive feedback. 		12 minutes
TP2 Explain the following five responsibilities of a follower in a team, to include:
a. assume responsibility;
b. be honest;
c. be inclusive and accept other team members;
d. know the assignment and coming prepared; and
e. clear communication with peers. 		15 minutes

Introduction

For the success of a team, each member must be cooperative and understanding of each other.

When a cadet understands the responsibilities of a follower, this helps them become more aware of what expectations are being set on them, which not only helps contribute to the team, but also as they progress through and eventually out of the cadet program as they begin to take on more responsibilities.
